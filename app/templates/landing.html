Upload your work schedule, and the smart scheduler automatically pairs trainees with qualified trainers across every shift. No more spreadsheets, no more guessing.

  • Blind schedule option to hide assignments until session time
  • Conflict detection prevents double-booking

Session Ratings

Detailed feedback on every skill, every session

Trainers rate trainees on each quality defined for the position. Color-coded 1-5 scales make it fast and intuitive. Optional comments let trainers call out specifics.

  • Custom skill descriptors per position (speed, accuracy, attitude, etc.)
  • Color-coded ratings from red (1) to green (5)
  • "Needs Attention" flag for manager follow-up
  • Full session history with score trends over time

Guest Trainer Mode

Anyone can train, no account needed

Miscommunication happens. When a new hire shows up and the scheduled trainer is out, Guest Trainer Mode saves the day. Generate a one-time link, share it by text, and anyone can submit a session evaluation.

  • QR code and shareable link generated in seconds
  • One-tap text message sharing via native SMS
  • Link expires after 24 hours or first use
  • Guest sessions clearly labeled in the system
