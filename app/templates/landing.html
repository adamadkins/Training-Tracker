Everything you need

A complete training system
for your whole team

From day one onboarding to position mastery, Training Tracker handles every step of the process.

Smart Scheduling

Build weekly training schedules in minutes. The smart scheduler automatically pairs trainees with qualified trainers and available positions.

Training Roadmaps

Define a structured path for each position. Assign trainees to roadmaps and watch them progress through every step, in order.

Progress Tracking

See exactly where each trainee stands across every position. Drill into session ratings, scores by quality, and trends over time.

Session Ratings

Trainers rate each session on multiple qualities like knowledge, speed, and attitude. Ratings are aggregated into a clear overall score.

Guest Trainer Mode

Need someone to train who is not in the system? Generate a one-time QR code link, share it by text, and they can submit a session without an account.

Team Messaging

Built-in direct messages and group channels so your team can communicate around training without needing a separate app.

Simple by design

Up and running in an afternoon

No IT team required. No six-week implementation. You set it up, your team starts using it.

1

Set up your positions

Add the positions at your location, define what good performance looks like for each one, and build training roadmaps for new hires.

2

Build your schedule

Create a weekly training schedule, assign sessions manually or let the smart scheduler do it, and publish it for your trainers to see.

3

Track and improve

Trainers submit ratings after each session. You see progress in real time, catch problems early, and know exactly when someone is ready to graduate.

Training Roadmaps

Every position has a clear path forward

Stop winging onboarding. Build a step-by-step roadmap for each position so every new hire gets the same quality of training, every time.

  • Ordered steps ensure trainees master basics before moving to advanced skills
  • Automatic progress tracking as sessions are completed and rated
  • Assign any trainee to a roadmap and track where they are at a glance
  • Know when they are ready to graduate and take on full responsibility
