Smart Scheduling

Build a full training week in one click

Upload your work schedule, and the smart scheduler automatically pairs trainees with qualified trainers across every shift.

Eliminate the spreadsheet shuffle

The Smart Scheduler reads your uploaded work schedule PDF, identifies who is working when, and auto-generates a training schedule that respects position qualifications and availability.

  • Upload PDF work schedules for automatic shift detection
  • Auto-pair trainees with qualified trainers
  • Blind schedule mode hides assignments until session time
  • Publish, unpublish, or delete entire weeks
  • Drag-and-drop manual adjustments

Session Ratings

Detailed feedback on every skill, every session

Custom criteria per position with color-coded 1–5 scales. Trainers submit evaluations in under two minutes.

Built for speed and specificity

Each position has its own set of rating criteria (speed, accuracy, guest interaction, etc.). Trainers tap a number, optionally leave a comment, and submit. The system aggregates scores automatically.

  • Custom skill descriptors per position
  • Color-coded 1–5 buttons (red to green)
  • Optional per-criteria comments
  • "Needs Attention" flag for manager follow-up
  • Overall notes for session-level context

Training Roadmaps

Every position has a clear path forward

Define ordered training steps for each position. Trainees must master each step before unlocking the next one.

Structured progression, not random sessions

Roadmaps ensure every trainee gets the same quality of training. Steps are completed in order, and the system automatically tracks where each person stands.

  • Define steps for each position (Register Basics → Order Taking → ...)
  • Steps lock/unlock automatically based on completion
  • Visual progress bar shows completion percentage
  • Minimum score threshold to advance
  • Manager can assign any trainee to a roadmap

Trainer Dashboard

  • Personal stat cards: Scheduled, Completed, Floor Active

  • Overdue sessions highlighted in red

  • Quick Train button for on-the-fly evaluations

Guest Trainer Mode

Anyone can train, no account needed

When the scheduled trainer calls out or you have a last-minute trainee, generate a one-time link and share it by text.

One-time links, zero friction

Generate a QR code or shareable link for any session. The guest trainer opens it on their phone, fills out the rating form, and submits. The evaluation appears in the system just like any other session.

  • QR code and copyable link generated instantly
  • One-tap SMS sharing via native text message
  • Link expires after 24 hours or first use
  • Guest sessions clearly labeled in the trainee's history
  • No login or account creation required
