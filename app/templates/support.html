Getting started

Training Tracker is built for restaurant and quick-service teams. Each company has its own secure portal (e.g. yourcompany.trainingtracker.me).

  • Managers set up employees, positions, roadmaps, and schedules.
  • Trainees view their schedule, complete sessions, and track progress.
  • Use the feature tour to see the product in action.
